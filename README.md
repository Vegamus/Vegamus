### What I do

I run **ILMIC LTD**, the technology arm behind a group of UK consultancies (immigration, business setup, education and property). Everything we sell to other businesses, we first run on ourselves: real leads, real deadlines, real compliance rules.

The problem we solve is simple: **small service firms lose money between the enquiry and the paid consultation.** Leads arrive from five channels, nobody replies for hours, follow-ups depend on memory, and nobody can see the pipeline. We fix that with small, boring, reliable systems.

### What we build

| Area | Typical build |
|---|---|
| **Speed-to-lead** | Form or ad lead → instant WhatsApp/email reply → qualification → booking link, within a minute |
| **CRM & pipeline** | One source of truth (Notion, HubSpot or Postgres), lead scoring, routing to the right person, chase cadences |
| **Paid consultation flow** | Booking, payment link, reminders, no-show recovery |
| **Compliance tracking** | Deadline alerts, document checklists and audit trails for regulated work (e.g. UK sponsor licence duties) |
| **AI assistants** | Document review, triage, reporting and drafting agents, with a human approving anything client-facing |
| **Ops monitoring** | Every workflow reports its own errors; one daily brief instead of fifty alerts |

### How we work

- **One source of truth.** Many forms are fine; one database is not negotiable.
- **Small first.** A manual process that works beats an automation nobody trusts. We automate the step that leaks the most money first.
- **Humans approve, machines prepare.** No AI sends regulated advice to a client.
- **GDPR by design.** Minimum data, UK/EU hosting where possible, no client data in logs or test fixtures.
- **Everything fails loudly.** If a workflow breaks, someone knows within minutes.

Company work lives in the **[ilmic-ltd](https://github.com/ilmic-ltd)** organisation. The patterns we reuse are written up in **[ilmic-automation-playbook](https://github.com/ilmic-ltd/ilmic-automation-playbook)**.
